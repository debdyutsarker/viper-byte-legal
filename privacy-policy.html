Privacy Policy – Viper Byte

Published by M.A.D.S. India · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Viper Byte is a retro-style Snake game that also contains a private, end-to-end encrypted chat between people who have approved each other as "buddies". The chat is opened with a secret key combination chosen by the user. Viper Byte is made and run by one person, Debdyut Sarker ("the developer"), publishing as M.A.D.S. India; in this policy "we" and "us" mean the developer. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. We built the app to collect as little as possible. Using the app also means agreeing to our Terms of Use.

The short version

  • No phone number, email address or contacts are needed or collected. Your identity in the chat is a random private ID made on your phone.
  • Messages and profile photos are end-to-end encrypted with the Signal Protocol. Our server only ever holds scrambled data it cannot read, and deletes each message once your buddy's phone has received it.
  • Your name and photo stay on your phone and are shared only with your buddies, encrypted.
  • Game data (scores, settings, medals) stays on your phone.
  • There are no ads, no advertising ID and no selling of data.

Proof with a report

When you report a buddy, the app asks "Add Messages as Proof?" with two choices, Add Last 10 Messages and Without Messages. Neither is picked for you. If you report one message (press and hold it > Report), that message and the 9 before it are added.

  • What the proof holds: the chat's last 10 messages as they are shown on your phone: text, stickers, replies, small copies of photos (at most 640 pixels) and the first frame of a video. Voice messages and view-once photos are not included, only a line saying one was there. It also holds both private IDs, the reason and the time.
  • How it is protected: it is sealed on your phone with the developer's public key (using libsignal, the same library as the chats), so our server holds only scrambled text it cannot open. Only the developer can open it, on the developer's own computer, with a private key kept offline.
  • It leaves your phone only if you choose it. If you pick Without Messages, no message content is sent.
  • The other person's messages: the proof includes messages the reported person sent you, shared without their consent. We allow this only to keep users safe and to stop unlawful content, as the DPDP Act 2023 permits for such "legitimate uses" and as the IT Rules 2021 require of us (due diligence on reported content).
  • What it can and cannot show: a changed copy of the app could fake messages, so proof is one thing we look at, never the only reason for a decision.
  • Kept: with the report, for up to 180 days, then deleted (see below).

Information that stays on your phone

  • Your messages, your buddies' names and photos, your own name and photo, your 12 secret words and private keys — kept in an encrypted database protected by Android's secure key storage.
  • Your secret combinations and app PIN — stored only as scrambled (salted and hashed) values, never as the combination or PIN itself.
  • Game data: high scores, medals, coins, chosen looks and settings.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a buddy's QR code or take a profile photo. Pictures are not saved to your gallery and are never uploaded readable; a profile photo is shared only with your buddies, end-to-end encrypted.

Features that use other services

  • Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging by Google): a silent signal tells your phone that new mail is waiting. It carries no message content or sender name, and the notification you see shows only a game-style text. This uses a device push token stored on our server.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics by Google): if the app crashes, a technical report (device model, Android version, the app's internal error details) is sent so we can fix it. It never includes your messages, names, photos or combinations.
  • Backup (optional): your game progress, and an encrypted copy of your chat, can be saved to your own Google Drive, in the app's private folder. The chat copy is locked with your 12 secret words, so only you can open it; we never receive either backup.
  • Purchases: the one-time Pro upgrade is bought through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google, not by us.

Google account data (backup)

Backup is optional. If you turn it on, you pick a Google account and allow one permission: access to the app's own folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). The app cannot see any other files in your Drive.

  • What the app uses: the account's email address and profile picture, shown on the Backup page so you know which account is in use; and the app's Drive folder, to save, find and restore your backups.
  • Where it stays: all of this is used only on your phone. The email address, profile picture, Drive access and backup files are never sent to our server or to anyone else.
  • Signing out: Sign Out in the Backup page gives back the app's Drive access and forgets the account and its picture on your phone. Your last backup stays in your Drive until you delete it (Google Drive > Settings > Manage apps), and Change Account removes this app's chat backup from the old account first.

Viper Byte's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent any information and do not share it with advertisers. Data is processed only by the service providers named above (Supabase for the chat server, and Google for notifications, crash reports, backup and purchases) to run the app. We may disclose information if required by law; since messages and photos are end-to-end encrypted, we cannot provide their content. The only exception is proof a reporter chose to add to a report: if it shows a crime, especially one involving a child, we may give it to the police or through cybercrime.gov.in as the law requires.

Security

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted in transit. Messages and photos are also end-to-end encrypted with the Signal Protocol. The server's database only allows each person to reach their own data.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Messages on the server are deleted as soon as they are delivered.
  • Deleting a chat or removing a buddy in the app removes that data from your phone.
  • You can delete your account and all server data linked to it from the app (Settings > Profile > Delete Account) or by emailing us at the address below; we do so within 30 days.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.
  • Reports, and any proof added to them, are kept for up to 180 days, to handle the report and for our legal duties (IT Rules 2021), then deleted. Proof involving a child that we pass to the police is kept only as long as needed for that.
  • A suspension record is deleted as soon as the suspension is lifted, or 180 days after it ended. It is kept until then even if the account is deleted, so deleting the account cannot be used to escape a suspension.
  • Security log entries are deleted automatically one year after the event (CERT-In asks for at least 180 days; India's data protection rules may ask for one year). They are kept for that time even if the account is deleted (the "account deleted" entry is itself one of them).
  • The Pro trial record (private ID and scrambled phone code) is kept after you delete your account, so that the same account or phone cannot start a second free trial. It contains no name, number or email and is used for nothing else.

Children

Viper Byte is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 (a child under India's DPDP Act); if you believe a child has used the chat, contact us and we will delete the account's server data.

The game can be played by anyone, but the chat is only for people aged 18 or over. Before the chat is set up, you tick "I'm 18 or Older". The tick is only checked on your phone at that moment; nothing about your age is stored or sent to us.
