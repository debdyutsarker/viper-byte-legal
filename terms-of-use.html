1. Who can use Viper Byte

  • The game can be played by anyone. It needs no account and works without the internet.
  • The chat is only for people aged 18 or over. Before the chat is set up, you confirm that you are 18 or over. Please do not use the chat if you are younger.
  • If we learn that a chat account belongs to someone under 18, we will close it and delete its server data.

2. Your chat account

  • Your account is a random private ID (for example SNK-7F3K-92QD) made on your phone from 12 secret words. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • You are responsible for your phone, your secret combination, your PIN and your 12 secret words. Keep them private. We never ask for them.
  • If you lose your 12 secret words and have no backup, nobody can bring the account back, not even us. This is part of how the app keeps your chat private.
  • An account is active on one phone at a time.

3. Buddies only

Viper Byte has no public chat, no groups and no way to find strangers. Two people can talk only after one sends a buddy request and the other accepts it. You can remove or block a buddy at any time, and you can report them (see section 5).

You must not use Viper Byte to send, share or ask for anything that:

  • is illegal in India or where you live;
  • sexually abuses or exploits a child in any way, including child sexual abuse material (we have zero tolerance for this; see our Child Safety Standards);
  • harasses, threatens, bullies, stalks or intimidates anyone;
  • shares someone's private or intimate pictures without their consent, or pretends to be someone else;
  • promotes violence, terrorism, or hatred against a group;
  • is spam, a scam, a fraud, a phishing link, or an attempt to take someone's money, account or data;
  • contains a virus or harmful code;
  • breaks someone else's copyright, trademark or other rights.

You must also not try to break, overload or get around the app's security, use someone else's account, or copy, sell or reverse-engineer the app.

5. Reports and what we can do

  • We cannot read your messages. Messages, photos, videos, voice messages, locations and contact cards are end-to-end encrypted. Our server only passes sealed boxes and deletes each one once it is delivered.
  • Report and Block: on a buddy's page, choose Report and pick a reason (Spam or Scam, Harassment or Threats, Sexual Content, Child Safety, Something Else). You can report and block in one step. We receive your private ID, their private ID, the reason and the time.
  • Proof (your choice): the app then asks "Add Messages as Proof?" (Add Last 10 Messages or Without Messages; neither is picked for you). Reporting one message (press and hold it > Report) adds that message and the 9 before it. The proof is the chat's last 10 messages as your phone shows them (photos made small; voice messages and view-once photos only noted), sealed on your phone so that only the developer can open it. It is kept with the report for up to 180 days. In the same way, messages you send may be added to a report by the buddy you send them to.
  • What we do with a report: the developer reviews it personally. Child-safety reports are handled first.
  • How decisions are made: we never suspend or close an account because of one report alone. We act only on a valid police or court order, or when 3 or more different people have reported the account within 30 days and the developer has looked at the proof they added. Proof cannot fully prove that a message was really sent (a changed app could fake it), so it is one thing we weigh, not the decision itself. Proof involving a child is passed to the police or cybercrime.gov.in as the law requires.
  • Suspension: we may suspend an account, for a set time or until lifted, without notice, when a court or government order lawfully asks us to, or for a serious breach of these terms (for example child sexual abuse, threats of violence, or fraud) found as described in "How decisions are made" above. A suspended account cannot send messages, send or accept buddy requests, or become anyone's buddy. It can still open the app, read its chats, block, report and delete the account. The app shows "This account is suspended until <date>" and the email address to write to for an appeal. The suspension is tied to the private ID, so reinstalling, restoring or deleting the account does not lift it. For serious breaches we may also close the account for good.
  • Appeal: if you think a suspension is wrong, write to the Grievance Officer (section 10) with your private ID and the reason. The developer reviews the appeal and replies within 15 days; a suspension found to be wrong is lifted at once. If you are still not satisfied, you can go to the Grievance Appellate Committee (section 10).
  • Lawful requests: we follow valid orders from Indian authorities. Because messages are end-to-end encrypted and deleted from our server after delivery, we can only share the little we hold (listed in the Privacy Policy), including any proof a reporter chose to add.

6. Pro, the free trial and coins

  • Free trial: each account and phone can try Pro free for 15 days, once. Nothing is charged when the trial ends; Pro simply turns off until you choose to buy it.
  • Price and refunds: Pro is not on sale yet. When it is, it is bought through Google Play, which shows the price, how long it lasts and how it renews before you buy, and lets you cancel at any time. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
  • Coins are earned only by playing the game and are spent only inside the game, on looks and other Earned items. Coins cannot be bought with money, have no money value, and can never be sold, transferred or cashed out. Viper Byte has no betting and no cash or prize rewards of any kind.
